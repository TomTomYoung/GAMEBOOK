Pass 1

Create one approximately 8:5 landscape illustration for a Japanese narrative gamebook. Very coarse low-color pixel art, large blocky pixels and broad flat color clusters, simple stepped silhouettes with roughly two tones per surface, sparse detail, strong graphic readability at 176 by 110 pixels. Treat each roof, rock, wall and cloud as a few large solid shapes. Calm composition, central subject within the middle 60 percent. Plain unlettered surfaces. A small postal harbor on a floating island above a bright sea of clouds. The main island is centered, with a timber mailhouse, simple slate pitched roofs, a short landing platform, a single plain ochre windsock and a winding footpath. The underside of the floating island is clearly visible as a large tapered rock mass with a few long roots hanging downward into open air. Two smaller distant islands sit at different depths beyond it. The cloud sea is built from broad cream and pale blue stepped bands, with clear blue sky above. Architecture and landscape fill the scene. Palette of airy blue, cream, muted teal, dark slate and warm ochre accents. The mood is a fresh working morning with room to breathe.
